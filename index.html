LEGAL & COMPLIANCE

Terms & Conditions

Core operating rules for NeoCity Miner.

1. Account

Each person is responsible for account security and truthful registration information. Abuse, automation, multi-accounting beyond the permitted limit, manipulation, or fraudulent activity may lead to suspension or blocking.

2. N Coin & H/s

N Coin is the internal reward currency. H/s is mining power. Production rewards, level changes, exchange results and wallet changes must be validated server-side.

3. Free Hashrate Spin

Cooldown is 3 hours. Normal reward range is 1–20 H/s. The 80 H/s welcome bonus is one-time only. Promotional jackpot visuals do not represent guaranteed large payouts.

4. Deposits & Withdrawals

Minimum deposit/top-up is $0.01. Withdrawal minimum is $1.00. Withdrawal eligibility requires cumulative qualifying top-up of at least $1.00. Payout is crypto-only and withdrawal requests remain subject to approval and fraud/security checks.

5. Offers, PTC & Referrals

Provider rewards are credited only after verified callbacks/events. Rewards should remain inside the configured economy budget and realized revenue limits.

6. Mining & Pools

Mining data may change with pool state, emission controls, account entitlements, energy rules and active economy parameters. Displayed estimates are not guaranteed payout promises.

7. Support

AI support provides informational assistance. It cannot approve financial transactions or override account/security controls.

PRIVACY & SECURITY

Privacy Center

How NeoCity handles account, support and financial security boundaries.

Data minimization

NeoCity should retain only data required for account, security, support and transaction operations.

Credentials & secrets

Service-role credentials, AI provider keys, payment-provider secrets and Turnstile secret keys are server-side only and must never appear in public frontend files.

Financial safety

Balances, rewards, withdrawals, deposits, exchanges and reward eligibility are authoritative only when accepted by the backend/database.

AI Support

AI Support is informational. It cannot approve withdrawals, edit balances, bypass security controls or override the account state stored by NeoCity.
